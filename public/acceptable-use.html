Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy applies to everyone who uses our tools or hosting, including people using a service a partner runs on our servers. Breaking it can lead to suspension or the end of a partnership.

You must not use our services to

  • break any law that applies to you or to us;
  • distribute malware, run phishing pages, or collect other people’s passwords or personal details;
  • send spam or other unsolicited bulk messages;
  • attack other systems, including denial-of-service attacks, port scanning, brute-forcing logins or exploiting vulnerabilities;
  • host or share child sexual abuse material, which we will report to the authorities;
  • infringe copyright or other intellectual property rights;
  • harass, threaten or publish private information about other people;
  • get around a suspension, or around limits we’ve set on your resources;
  • resell or share access to our servers without our agreement.

Proxies

If you run a proxy on our servers, you’re responsible for how it’s used. Don’t let it be used to hide any of the activity above. If we get abuse reports about traffic from your service, we’ll ask you to deal with them, and may limit the service while that happens.

Fair use of resources

Our servers are shared between partners. If your project uses enough CPU, memory, storage or bandwidth to affect others, we’ll talk to you about it first. If it’s urgent, we may limit it before we get the chance.

Reporting abuse

If you think something on our servers breaks this policy, email support@axisnet.work with as much detail as you can, such as URLs, IP addresses, times and what happened.

How we enforce this

We look at each case on its own. Depending on how serious it is, we may ask you to fix the problem, remove content, suspend services, or end the partnership. For the most serious cases, like child abuse material or credible threats of violence, we act immediately without notice and report to the relevant authorities.
