How working with us goes

We’d rather look after a few projects properly than a lot of them badly. So we pick who we work with, and we talk to the people we host.

Proxy tools

Web proxy software we write and maintain ourselves. The source is public, and you can read through it on our projects page.

Hosting

Room on our Sydney servers for your project, set up by us and kept running by us.

  1. Get in touch

    Send us a message on Discord once it opens, or email us now. Tell us who you are and what you want to run.

  2. We talk it through

    If it’s a good fit for both sides, we agree on what we’ll provide and what we expect in return.

  3. You’re set up

    We get your project running. When something breaks, you message us directly, not a ticket queue.
