01Partners, not clients

We don’t have clients. We have partners: people whose projects we want to see running. We choose who we work with, host them on our own servers, and when something breaks they talk to us, not a ticket queue.

A

Proxy tools

Web proxy software we write, run and maintain ourselves.

B

Hosting

Room on our Sydney servers for your project, set up and kept running by us.

How it works

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Message us on our Discord or email us. Tell us who you are and what you want to run.

  2. 02

    We talk it through

    If it’s a good fit for both sides, we agree on what we’ll provide and what we expect in return.

  3. 03

    You’re set up

    We get your project running. When something breaks, you message us directly.
